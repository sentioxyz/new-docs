## Metadata

After the processor is successfully uploaded, you can first check the status of the processor on [**Datasource**](/the-monitoring) tab. Make sure the status is shown as <span style={{ color: "green" }}>**PROCESSING**</span>. Also, take notice of the **block number** or **Version** on each chain (Yes, we support multi-chain: [multi-chain-support](/multi-chain)) is processed at.

![Viewing Data Source](/assets/datasource.gif)

You can also examine the status of the collected [metrics](/metrics) on the Metrics tab.

![Viewing Metrics](/assets/image-5-4-1.png)

Clicking on each [metric](/metrics) gives you a more detailed view of the metric, including tags and visualization of the metrics.
