<Steps>
<Step>

### Sign in to Sentio Connect

Open [connect.sentio.xyz](https://connect.sentio.xyz) and sign in. No API key is needed.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add a wallet

On **Home**, click **Add wallet**. Enter an EVM address (`0x…`), an ENS name (`name.eth`) or a Sui address, pick one or more chains, and click **Add wallet**.

![Add wallet dialog](/assets/connect/add-wallet.jpg)

Each wallet appears under **Your wallets**, and its balances and DeFi positions load below. You can also skip this step and ask your assistant to add wallets for you later.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add Sentio Connect to your assistant

Add a custom connector (MCP server) with this URL:

```text
https://connect.sentio.xyz/v1/mcp
```

Your assistant opens the Sentio Connect consent page. Click **Allow connection**. See [Connect your assistant](/connect/connect-your-assistant) for step-by-step instructions for Claude, ChatGPT, Codex and Grok.

</Step>
<Step>

### Ask a question

Start a new conversation and try:

> What's the total value of my tracked wallets, and which tokens make up most of it?

The assistant calls `get_portfolio_value` and `get_wallet_balances` and answers with live data. In Claude and ChatGPT, balances and DeFi positions also render as interactive cards right in the conversation.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Example prompts

| Ask | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| "List my wallets." | `list_wallet_addresses` |
| "What do I hold on Base?" | `get_wallet_balances` for your wallets on chain `8453` |
| "What's the price of SUI?" | `get_token_price` by symbol |
| "Show my Aave and Uniswap positions." | `get_defi_positions` |
| "How did my WETH change over the past week?" | `get_token_balance_history` with `range: 7d` |
| "Track `0x…` on Ethereum and Arbitrum." | `add_wallet_address` (needs write access) |
| "Stop tracking my Sui wallet." | `remove_wallet_address` — most assistants ask you to confirm first |
